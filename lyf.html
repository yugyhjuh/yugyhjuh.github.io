Overview

Designed art assets and gameplay prototype for an AR filter game commissioned by Ascott Group for LYF One-North. Players tilt their head to dodge or collect recyclables — the game was designed to promote sustainability awareness in a playful, interactive format.

My Role
01 2D Art & Asset Design – Designed all in-game art assets in Krita, including characters, collectibles, obstacles, and UI elements.
02 Concept Ideation – Produced early concept sketches to establish the visual direction before moving into final asset production.
03 UI Prototype – Built a clickable UI prototype in Adobe XD covering the game's onboarding and screen flow.
