Design Iterations

Iteration 1 & 2 - Early concepts focused on lifting the mattress and clamping it to secure the sheet during tucking. Iteration 1 used a flat platform while Iteration 2 introduced a scissor-lift for better stability - preferred as the flat platform required excessive clearance height when fully lowered.

A top-mounted sensor was added to track the housekeeper's position and move the device accordingly. However, we identified a key limitation: repositioning after lifting caused previously tucked sheets to come undone, making this approach counterproductive.

Other design decisions included a detachable ramp for easier replacement and storage, and a cushioned surface for users to apply downward pressure comfortably. The side-button locking mechanism was later flagged as unintuitive and ergonomically challenging under load.

Prototype

The prototype uses an Arduino Uno with 3 motor drivers - 2 for the wheel pairs and 1 for the linear actuator. Directional buttons control both the side wheels and actuator height, with worm gear motors driving the wheels.

Outcome
Result
LionsBot responded positively to the assistive design approach, noting a noticeable strength difference when using the prototype hands-on.
Key Learning
Using a Mixamo character as a visual reference before prompting AI image tools significantly improved output accuracy - particularly for hand placement and ergonomic positioning.
