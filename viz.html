Overview

A credit-bearing internship at Viziofly Pte Ltd, working as a 3D artist on Metascape City - an in-development game set in a stylised, cyber-tech Singapore. My primary role was modelling Singapore buildings and game assets in Blender, alongside client work for CNA and RSAF. Beyond modelling, I also did level design for the enemy level areas and occasionally assisted with Unity development - adding responsive UI elements like enemy health bars and fixing boundary issues.

3D Models

Before starting, I was advised to work through the Polygon Runway tutorial to get familiar with Blender and the low-poly aesthetic the company was going for. Over the course of the internship, I modelled 55+ buildings and various game assets. Building textures were created in Photoshop; asset textures like food glazing were done through Blender nodes. The work shown here is a small sample - the files belong to the company so only screenshots are available.




Level Design

Designed around 9 enemy level areas in Unity using given prefabs, blocking out terrain, enemy placement and types, and spatial flow. Some featured parkour sections and underground areas, all designed to give players both a place to explore and farm materials and EXP.

Clients
CNA
01
Raphael - Remembering Me

A CNA documentary project about a father reconnecting with his late son through a VR experience. I used CC4 to edit and customise a base character model - adjusting facial features, adding distinguishing details like moles, and modelling of his glasses. Review meetings were held with his father and CNA to ensure likeness accuracy. The character was then brought into iClone8 for lip-syncing and interaction animations, before being integrated into Unity to assign the respective scripts for the VR integration.

02
Chor Meng - Remembering Me

A CNA documentary episode exploring how technology can preserve a performer's craft beyond physical limitations. Chew Chor Meng, best known as Mr. Kiasu, is battling Kennedy's disease which has affected his ability to act. The project digitally recreated his likeness and animated it to allow him to perform again through a virtual avatar. My role followed the same pipeline as Raphael - character customisation in CC4 and acting animations and lip-syncing in iClone8.
